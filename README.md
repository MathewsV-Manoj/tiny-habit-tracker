# Tiny Habit Tracker

> A simple place to notice the little things you are trying to do more often.

## How to use it

- Choose one or two habits, not a whole life makeover.
- Mark the days you showed up in any form.
- Look for patterns with curiosity rather than judgment.

## A small note to yourself

Consistency is useful, but being kind to yourself is useful too.
